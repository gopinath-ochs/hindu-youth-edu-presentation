About the Ṣaḍdarśana

The six orthodox (āstika) schools of Hindu philosophy each accept the authority of the Vedas, yet arrive at strikingly different accounts of the self, reality, knowledge, and liberation. Studied together, they form a map of the possible answers to the deepest human questions.
