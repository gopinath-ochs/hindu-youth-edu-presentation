What is Śāstra?

Every Hindu tradition inherits the same vast archive of texts — but each chooses a different centre of gravity. One school's supreme revelation is another's secondary supplement. Select a tradition to see how it defines its own sacred canon.

Śruti vs. Smṛti vs. Āgama: Classical tradition distinguishes "heard" revelation (Vedas), "remembered" tradition (Purāṇas, Itihāsa), and "received" texts (Āgamas, Tantras). In practice, most traditions elevate at least one non-Vedic text to the status of supreme authority.
